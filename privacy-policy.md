# TimeTracker Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 14, 2026

## 1. Introduction

TimeTracker is an employee time-tracking application operated by Advance Framing Materials. This Privacy Policy explains how TimeTracker collects, uses, stores, and protects information when employees use the application.

## 2. Information We Collect

TimeTracker may collect and process the following information:

- **Account information:** Employee username, account identifiers, and authentication tokens.
- **Employee information:** Employee name and employee ID associated with an account.
- **Timekeeping records:** Clock-in times, clock-out times, and work-hour history.
- **Technical information:** Information necessary for secure server communication and authentication, such as connection logs and IP addresses, where recorded by the server.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Authenticate employees and maintain secure login sessions.
- Record and display employee clock-in and clock-out times.
- Provide access to individual time-entry histories.
- Maintain accurate timekeeping records.
- Maintain, troubleshoot, and secure the application and its server.

TimeTracker does not use employee information for targeted advertising.

## 4. Data Storage and Sharing

Information submitted through TimeTracker is transmitted to our server at api.advanceframingmaterial.com for authentication and timekeeping services.

Employee information is accessible only as needed to provide and administer the service. We do not sell personal information. We may use hosting or infrastructure providers to operate the service, and those providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security of the service.

## 5. Data Security

We use HTTPS for communication between the application and our server. Authentication tokens are stored using appropriate secure storage mechanisms where supported.

We take reasonable measures to protect employee information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Employee account information and timekeeping records are retained as necessary to operate the service and meet applicable business and legal recordkeeping requirements.

Employees who want to access, correct, or request deletion of their personal information should contact the application administrator using the contact information below. Requests may be subject to applicable legal and employment recordkeeping requirements.

## 7. Children's Privacy

TimeTracker is intended for workplace timekeeping and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the application.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the application or its data practices change. The latest version will be made available at the published privacy policy address, with an updated effective date when appropriate.
